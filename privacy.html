Privacy Policy

DocExpire Reminder

Last updated: Nov 06, 2026

DocExpire Reminder respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the DocExpire Reminder mobile application (the “App”).

Information We Collect

The App is designed to work offline and does not collect personal data through its core functionality.

We do not collect, store, or share through our own servers:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Physical addresses
  • Identification documents
  • Document expiration details

Any reminder data you create, such as document titles, categories, expiration dates, and notes, is stored locally on your device.

We do not have access to your locally stored reminder data, and it is not uploaded to our servers.

Notifications

The App uses local notifications to remind you about upcoming document expiration dates.

  • Notifications are generated and managed entirely on your device
  • No reminder or notification content is sent to our servers

You can manage notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

Subscriptions and Payments

The App offers subscription-based access to certain features. Subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store and managed using RevenueCat.

RevenueCat may process purchase-related information, including transaction identifiers, subscription status, app user identifiers, and information required to verify subscription entitlements and provide subscription access.

Apple processes payments and may handle payment information, including payment card details. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

Subscriptions renew automatically according to the selected plan unless canceled in accordance with Apple's subscription terms.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple Account settings. Eligible purchases may be restored through the App.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.

The App uses the following services when necessary:

  • Apple App Store: Processes subscription payments and purchase transactions.
  • RevenueCat: Supports subscription management, purchase validation, and entitlement verification.

These services may process information required to provide subscription functionality. Reminder data, document details, and expiration dates are not shared with RevenueCat for subscription management.

Data Security

Reminder data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. The App does not operate an external database for storing your documents or reminders.

The security of locally stored information depends on your device's security settings. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13, or the minimum age required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
