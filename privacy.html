Privacy Policy

DocExpire Reminder

Last updated: March 1, 2026

DocExpire Reminder respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the DocExpire Reminder mobile application (the “App”).

Information We Collect

The App is designed to work without collecting personal data.

We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Physical addresses
  • Identification documents
  • Payment information

Any reminder data you create (such as titles, dates, and notes) is stored locally on your device.

Notifications

The App uses local notifications to remind you about upcoming expiration dates.

  • Notifications are generated and managed entirely on your device
  • No notification data is sent to external servers

You can manage notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.

No data is shared with third parties.

Data Security

Since the App does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no external storage of user information. Your reminder data remains under your control on your device.

Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
