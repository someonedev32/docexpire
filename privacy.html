Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-28
Contact: support@inovation.app

Summary: This app works fully offline. We do not collect, store, share, or sell personal data. Your document entries stay on your device.

1) Data We Collect

We collect no personal data.

The app does not use accounts, servers, cloud sync, or any database controlled by us. Any document information you enter (e.g., document name/type, expiration date, notes) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

2) Network & Third-Party Services

The app does not send your data to any external server. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking. If you open external links (e.g., this policy page), those websites may collect data under their own policies.

3) Notifications (Reminders)

If you enable reminders, the app uses local notifications on your device to alert you before an expiration date. We do not access, read, or upload your notification content.

4) Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app typically removes the local data from your device. (Device backups are controlled by your operating system settings.)

5) Sharing

We do not share your information because we do not collect it.

6) Security

Since data remains on your device, your security depends on your device protections (passcode/biometrics). We recommend enabling device security features.

7) Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children because the app does not collect any data at all.

8) Changes to This Policy

If the app’s functionality changes (for example, adding cloud sync or analytics), we will update this policy accordingly. The effective date above reflects the latest version.
